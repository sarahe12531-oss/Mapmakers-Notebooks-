✨ How to Weave ✨

The Formula of Magic

In Atheria, a weave is the careful alignment of frequencies, rather than a spontaneous burst of power. The spoken word is the easiest way to create the resonance needed to weave.

To weave resonance, you must utilize three distinct components:

-The Fuel (Resonance & Intent),
-The Vehicle (The Words),
-The Weather (Celestial influence).
Mastery of these ensures your magic moves as it's supposed to and avoids damaging the weaver, backfiring obnoxiously, or creating dissonance that attracts entities like Gremlins or Scatter-Souls.

The Fuel (Resonance & Intention):
Everyone's natural resonance is necessary in the ingredients of the weave. One can tune with the frequency of the Rootmother River, using both sources of resonance to power the weave. Then it is projected physically through the tone of your voice.
- Low tones are for grounding and stabilizing (mending objects, sonic wards, physical anchors).
- Mid tones are used in illusion and shaping weaves (communication, resonance shaping, neutral projections).
-High tones activate and release weaves that summon, cause energy spikes, and initiate movement.

The Vehicle (The Words):
This gives the weave its form. Magic is a collaborative acoustic act. Resonance in Atheria responds more openly to a "Standard Pitch." Atheric was crafted to ensure that diverse cultures can weave together without creating harmonic static. Weaving in one's native tongue is used for ancestral rites, private healing, and familial bonds. These weaves are emotionally nuanced, mostly tonal sounds, and rich with historical & ancestral resonance. When multiple cultures weave together, weaving is done in Atheric. It is the "common score" that allows weavers from the Twin-Currents to the Rootwood to contribute to a singular, stable weave without causing dissonance.

The Weather (Celestial Influence):
The planets, moons, and celestial currents represent the "road conditions." Their position in the sky determines how magic behaves. The five planets and sun determine the cost and consequence of your magic. The moons govern volatility and emotion. Together, they determine how the magic "feels." Finally, the Currents are the patterns in our lives that dictate where magical energy pools, flows, or knots.

The Septenary of Echoes

The Wheels do not turn. Turning is a Celestiran notation describing changes in the aggregate relationship between immeasurable forces. The Wheels are not physical objects, nor do they command the forces whose names they bear. They are the language Celestira gave to patterns too vast and numerous for mortal minds to perceive whole. Each represents countless small movements occurring across existence at once: choices made, roads crossed, memories retained, chances taken, ages ending. Celestira reads these movements as easily as mortals might read ink.
She writes what she sees into the heavens in Divine Atheric. Astriel, her Envoy, translates her message. Even translated, much of its meaning exceeds ordinary mortal comprehension. Skilled Oneiroi and celestial scholars spend lifetimes learning to read what remains. Thus, when stargazers say the Wheels are turning, grinding, or aligned, they are describing the condition of reality, not the motion of celestial machinery.

They have been given many names. And depending on who is speaking, any number of which you will hear. The most widely used are, The Shifting Times, the Orrery of Breath, the Counterwise Clock.

The Wheels I - IV

The Outer Wheel of Fate:
Trajectories
The accumulated direction of things. At the smallest level, practically invisible. Across civilizations, worlds and ages, those tiny trajectories become enormous momentum. That's why it is slow and relentless and why it creaks around collapsing empires. The Wheel does on the large scale for all existence what Syric does for Atheria. Syric works with Atheria's threads. The Wheel registers all threads everywhere. There may be many realms like Atheria and they may all have a Syric.
The Second Wheel of Chance:
Deviations:
Slightly off-center. Always has been. It describes the countless tiny possibilities by which something might happen differently than expected. A wrong turn, a misplaced step, a seed carried one field farther, a meeting is delayed by a moment. It is a weave catching an unintended note. Most deviations disappear unnoticed. Others redirect entire lives. Its famous wobble is Celestiran notation for the persistent uncertainty woven through existence. It is why miracles happen. It is also why excellent plans fail beautifully.
The Third Wheel three Seasons:
Cycles:
Not merely weather. Not merely years. The Third Wheel describes cycles of becoming: emergence, flourishing, decline, ending and renewal. Forests have seasons. Civilizations have seasons. Magic has seasons. Ideas, creatures, stories and even the things mortals consider permanent may eventually reach winter. Its smallest movements occur constantly and everywhere. Its greatest shifts can define an age. When the Third Wheel turns heavily, old myths retire and new monsters learn their names.

The Fourth Wheel of Topsides & Roads:
Connections and crossings:
Everywhere has a way in. Not every way remains open. The Fourth Wheel describes crossings. The movement between places, states and boundaries. Footpaths and sea lanes belong to it, but so do migrations, thresholds, messages, currents and stranger passages for which mortal cartographers have no satisfactory symbols. Sailors say it hums before storms. Travelers blame it when familiar roads feel strangely wrong. When its relationships grind against the other Wheels, maps have been known to twitch. Cartographers generally prefer not to discuss this.

The Wheels IV - VII

The Fifth Wheel of Memory:
Retention: Most things travel forward. Memory occasionally disagrees. The Fifth Wheel describes what existence retains. The resonance left by something that was once present and the innumerable ways the past continues to participate in the present. Celestiran notation sometimes depicts this Wheel as moving backward. Scholars still disagree about precisely what this means but they do agree on the impact it leaves. Ghosts feel warm. Forgotten songs return complete, and names come back to the living uninvited.

The Sixth Wheel of Will:
Choices:
The most fragile the most frequently touched. The Sixth Wheel describes choice. Every deliberate act exerts an almost immeasurably small pressure upon it. Most mortals will never know they have touched anything at all. But there are a great many mortals. Every action of refusal, each small kindness, a betrayal, a traveler's decision to stay, the homebody's decision to leave. Individually, each moves almost nothing. Together, the choices we make cause a cosmic ripple effect. Some create waves, but it's often the smallest creatures in creation that collectively have the greater influence over the architecture of existence.

The Hidden Wheel Silence:
Absence:
Unlike the other Wheels, Silence has never been translated by archivists who can agree on whether or not the translation is correct. Silence isn't automatically absence, death, Void, forgetting or rest in Atheria. The fact that nobody can properly chart this Wheel may be exactly why nobody agrees on what it is measuring.
Mortals and gods alike both experience the impact. Reading them are not predictions. They are a diagnosis. “Ah,” the stargazers say, “the wheels are grinding today. Be gentle with promises” Magic works best when wheels align and backfires when they grind.

The Moons

While planets set the "road conditions," the three moons: Selunaris, Veyra, and Kalthyr, govern the Volatility and Emotion of the weave.
Selunaris: (The Silver Moon): The “main” moon. Governs tides, dreams, healing, intuition. It is a masterpiece for healing and dreamwalking, but it lacks the sharp attack needed for destructive casting. This is the most “natural” and intuitive celestial body for mages to harmonize with.
Veyra: (The Amethyst Moon): The moon of shifting perspectives and prophetic dissonance. It governs illusions and emotional intensity. It is famously unstable; under its amethyst light, the “truth” often bends or vibrates with a dangerous, unpredictable intensity. Excellent for astral work and glamours, but dangerous for binding oaths that require precision.
Kalthyr: (The Ashen Moon): The Underworld moon. The moon of the cold, unvarnished truth of the world beneath. It governs spirits and memory with a sharp, clear resonance that lacks any emotional padding or comforting overtones. “cuts” clean and deep. It is the premier moon for truth rites and the severing of ties, as forgotten things resurface with clinical clarity; however, it is a poor choice for sustaining long-term enchantments.

The People and Magic

Every race weaves by the same laws. They use the same formula, the same Standard Pitch called Atheric, and tune to the same Atherian frequency. What differs is the resonance they bring into the braid. Each race possesses its own natural frequency. As that frequency braids with Atheria's, it gives the resulting weave a characteristic flavor. The flavor does not determine what can be woven; it influences how the weave manifests.

A levitation weave remains a levitation weave whether performed by a Vespralith, Tide-Runner, Mycelian, or Shardborn. The words and required tones may be identical, but the resulting weaves will carry subtle differences because the frequency entering the braid is different. In group weaving, participants first tune to one another. Their individual frequencies form a shared chord, which is then brought into tune with Atheria. The completed weave therefore carries the flavors of every resonance contributing to it. Atheric standardizes the score. It does not standardize the singer.
