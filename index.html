The Vein Beneath the World

Black Lantern University

This Shroudspire institution is devoted to the teaching of Serisyn as goddess of death, mirrors and transformations. Their founding is of unusual circumstances. All six had either near death experiences or were closely tied to River by profession or familiar ties. After the founding, all six would return to the river. They would not get a second departure.

The Six Founders & Five Rites

I. The Founder Who Heard Mercy:
He returned believing mercy was permission to remain unfinished.
II. The Founder Who Heard Judgment:
She returned with terror in her mouth and doctrine in her hands,
speaking as though truth must always wound in order to matter.
III. The Founder Who Heard Silence:
He returned devoted to hush, to restraint, to the sacredness of not speaking too quickly.
IV. The Founder Who Heard Exposure:
She returned fierce in her hatred of pretense, determined to tear lies from every soul she met.
V. The Founder Who Heard Restoration:
Innocence, was not what had brought him back. It was the terrible possibility that even after distortion, something true could still be mended.
VI. The Founder Who Heard Warning:
What she heard in the river was not doom, but caution. A warning, she made sure to heed.

Reading Path & A Word With Gods

Echorin Vareth

Known as the The Rootmother's last note and the First Listener, Echorin is a savior tasked with reverberating the original Song of Lysera. He is currently searching for the ten lost notebooks that map the resonance of Elsewhere. Echorin does not just remember; he "reverberates." He maps emotional resonance and acts as a living tuning fork to bring lost souls like Vaessa and Arenox home. He is what Arenox could have been: a being who witnesses the wound without being consumed by the bleeding.

The First Listener

Followed by not one, but two gnawing entities. The Song, that guides him and, a more sinister being, the Earworm. Insatiable and irresistable that grew from himself then crawled in throuh his ear to learn his posture. Often tempting and succeeding to lead him astray. Despite this, at heart, Echorin is defined by attention. He listens to rivers, to silence, to grief, to memory, and to the emotional shape places leave behind. He does not map geography in the ordinary sense. He maps the emotional impact of places and people. Its resonance or the echo a place leaves in a heart, the sorrow caught in a road, the tenderness that clings to a ruin, the way a field remembers joy after devastation. For him, the truest landscape is emotional and spiritual rather than physical.

Obligitory Preface Statement

I am told this sort of introduction is customary so here is my feeble attempt at an intro. This site is made for wider screen sizes but I try my best to make every page both mobile and desktop friendly. This is a site is something I've been working on in my head for awhile and finally decided to actually get it out. Atheria has been a way for me to discover things about myself I didn't know before. I've loved writing for as long as I can remember, I guess its something I felt naturally good at, and I don't have many of those. Mostly its just a random assortment of pages I've attempted to connect to each other. I've always been a poet, that came easy to me, long narrative writing was always a challenge for me but its getting easier I believe. If you are the wandering adventuring type, honestly just pick a page and wing it. You never know what each page has. For straight up prose, head to the First Listener page or the Creation story pages. The country pages also have some vignettes. This is a sandbox, go have fun just don't eat the sand, its not a treat. I'm going to slip into my bureaucratic zoot suit now. You matter. You are loved 🖤


Atheria, was born of a want shaped desire and is currently held together by an incredibly stressed cartographer. It's strongly recommended that you adjust your expectations and lower your standards. If you're looking for a carefully structured high fantasy with a recommended reading order, I'm afraid you've wandered into the wrong forest. There isn't one, the 'high' might just be the only thing I can guarantee right now. I can't give you rigid structure, or a single straight path to follow. My pretzel brain simply won't allow it. What I can offer, is a sandbox to explore, a new perspective, genuine empathy, and the humor in any situation.

I've learned that we can't cut away the pieces of ourselves that we dislike and still call it healing. I've learned that I can be all of me, all of the time. I've learned that its ok to express my jumbled thoughts that make my brain a pretzel. Soft, a little salty and covered in cheese. Atheria isn't asking for perfection. It only asks that you hold all of you honestly. All your messy, sorrowfull, beautifully unhinged self all at once. And you still call it love.


📜 Unecessary Rambles ✦

The Vow of Inquiry:
"A well-framed question carries more power than an unexamined answer.
If you debate to reach a victory, you have already lost."
Atheria isn't interested in telling you which emotions are good and which are bad. Anger, shame, grief, joy, fear, envy, none of them are villains. They're whispers, trying to tell you something. Sometimes they're terrible teachers. Sometimes they're faithful guides. Denying them rarely makes them disappear. Usually they just become less visible....until you catch your reflection retreating in the mirror. Until your shadow lags three paces behind. What you deny in yourself, will one day call your name in another’s voice.
I believe most things, are forgivable, if reconciliation is truly the goal. When you hold onto grudges, you aren't burying just one person. Both of you feel its weight. Ask yourself why you're holding it. Ask why until why is exhausted. Why leads to understanding.
The lives of these people are driven by a desire for answers to the fundamental questions that perhaps we've all asked ourselves at one point or another.

Those answers hold the key to the meaning of life in Atheria.

How do you recognize when you are lying to yourself?
What does it really mean to understand another person?
How do you face grief without making it your identity?
How do you tell the truth without using it as a weapon?
Can you hear another soul without trying to possess it?
Perhaps, the most important question, every Atherian must answer.
When you take the river ride in the Vein Beneath the World,
Serisyn, goddess of death asks but one question, you cannot answer falsely.

Are you aligned with yourself?

Atheria definitely lacks structure and in some places restraint. It doesn't follow any pre-set rules. Though, following the rules has never been something I was good at.
This has been slowly writing itself inside my head since before I knew how to write. Recently years have been a series of decisions made in attempt to discover myself. Some good ones, others not so much. Throughout every choice, good or bad, I learned many things about me. Some things may be frowned upon by more sophisticated society. However, I never did learn to care about what other's thought about me.
Throughout these pages, you'll find lore, character driven narratives, poetry, and at times my thoughts of rants and ramblings. You can't get lost, if you don't know where you're going, and so, every path is correct. Each page was heavily inspired by Jungian psychology, various philosophers, string theory, and a deeply held belief that despite the pain, self loathing, and the impossible living standards, that there is good in this world. And it I believe it is worth fighting for. Maybe this whole thing sucks. I doubt anyone is reading this anyway.
