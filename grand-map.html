The Astral Plains (Northwest)

Creator: Cross-ties Goddess of stars, dreams, and the astral world.

The Caretakers of the Psyche: The Oneiroi are charged with the sacred duty of reading the cosmos, protecting the Astral world, and organizing specialized factions aligned with the cosmic Pillars. Their primary magical focuses encompass cosmic manipulation, dream walking, and astral charting.

🌘 Astral Plains

The Shardborn Territories (Far North)

Origin: Beings formed from leftover fragments of resonance from the gods. Born from geodes, they practice resonance weaving through prismatic light waves.

Physiology & Society: Characterized by porcelain white or ashen skin with physical crystals embedded in their bodies that hum with strong emotion, alongside veins that glow with a faint geometric light. A divergent branch features charcoal-colored skin and small horns. Their cities are built directly inside giant crystal geodes, and they use readable sound tones instead of written language for history. Their magic can uniquely amplify the power of other races, risking internal fractures if overwhelmed.

🌘 Shardborn Territories

Auralith (Northeast)

Creator: Virelia, Goddess of Communication, Knowledge, and Wisdom.

The Resonant Philosophers: A land of twilight forests and black-sand seas experiencing the copper-blue dusk known as Vespertide. Humanoids here are tall and willow-slim with storm-slate or deep indigo skin. Masters of Resonance Weaving and sonomancy, their golden bioluminescent lumen-tracery veins and tuning-notched ears serve as absolute conduits to perceive and manipulate ambient vibrations. Governed by the Luminous Concord.

🌘 Auralith

The Twin Currents Archipelago (Southeast)

Creators: Syric (God of Fate) and Aria (Goddess of Luck).

The Weavers of Chance: A fluid maritime chain of islands that perceives the universe as a Great Tapestry woven from the fixed warp threads of fate and the colorful weft threads of choice and chance. Their capital, Port Probability, fluidly adapts its layout to changing tides. Their probability magic ranges from immediate luck nudging to advanced Pathweaving.

The Twin Currents

The Rootwood (Southwest)

Creator: Sylvara, Goddess of all things that grow, renewal, and rebirth ("Finest Blooms").

The Guardians of Vitality: Slender, Fae-like beings with pale lavender skin, bioluminescent spots, and delicate, translucent insectoid wings. Their values center on grace and environmental renewal. Their architecture is grown and cultivated into giant Mushroom Houses, clustered in cities like Sporehaven. Their defensive military force, the Thallus Guard, utilizes non-lethal spores to safely disable invaders.

Rootwood

Sunstone Dominion (Central-West)

Creator: Alezion, God of Balance, Justice, and Equality.

The Architects of Balance: A statuesque and graceful realm of floating islands carved from luminous pale blue stone. Humanoids boast pearlescent skin with eyes radiating inner light and embedded Living Light Crystals. They specialize in the trade of magical Skyglass, Sky-Fishing the cloud seas, and command magic like Cloudwalk, Cloudspeech, and Sun's Spark.

Shroudspire Peaks & Factions

Hovering precariously above the Oxymere, these massive peaks are knit together by dense forest canopies.

⚠️ Balanced Co-Equal Factional Notice

Eighteen years ago, the Shadow Clan split into two completely separate, equal cultures following a core philosophical dispute. They operate independently as non-subordinate entities.

The Silent Weavers

An introverted, neutral philosophical faction dedicated to quiet contemplation, operating Black Lantern University. They interact with shadows as companions.

The Blackwake Couriers

Pragmatic and hardened shadow manipulators. They command shadow transportation networks while strictly avoiding the sun.

The Respite & Songfields

The Neutral Zone: The crossroads created for the drafting of the Treaty of Ink and Men, housing major guildhalls, the Resonance Accord, and Gendarme HQ.

The Irisireph: Ethereal tuners born directly of Echorin's essence who command specialized music and vibration magic to serve as baseline structural tuners.

The Unseeing: Formerly known as the Sighted Blood. Stripped of natural sight by the gods, they adapted beautifully to navigate via tactical vibration and absorb resonance on their own terms.
